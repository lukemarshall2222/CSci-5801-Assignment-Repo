The classes represented in the Rocket Launch system are the control pad, the lanch unit, and the 
rocket. The launch unit includes the launch pad, the igniter, and the battery, 
communicates with the control pad with enable and launch confirm operations, and causes the rocket to
launch. The control pad interfaces with the user, and contains the interface and operations to ready 
and launch the rocket, communicating these operations to the control unit. The rocket is the rocket 
body and engine, and is ignited by the igniter in the launch pad.
