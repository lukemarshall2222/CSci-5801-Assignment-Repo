The key architectural components of the RLS are represented in the following:
- The ignition system
  - Misfires or errors in the igniter circuit/battery can result in serious injury or terrifying unknown situations. Inconsistency here is dangerous and makes the system less useful
- The control unit
  - The control unit represents the primary vector of user interactions, so configuration of this unit must be understandable and consistent for optimal usability. It's hardware must be robust.
- The launch pad
  - the launch pad is expected to handle to dangerous parts so must act consistently especially in the case of failure. 
- Control Unit/Launchpad interface
  - The latency and consistency of communication between units is very significant. Errors can lead to confusion from the user or dangerous conditions. Communication must be intuitive to the user for easy diagnosis of physical issues.
